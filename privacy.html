Privacy policy

Household mini is a private tool used only by the members of one household. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it reads

With each household member's permission, it reads that person's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Contacts and Google Drive files. It can create files in Google Drive and change only the files it created itself.

Why

The data is used only to run the household's own schedule and records: finding school dates, activity changes and reminders, and keeping them in one place for the family.

Where it is kept

Data is stored on the household's own computer and in an encrypted backup in the household's own Google Drive. To read and sort items, content is processed by Anthropic's Claude under the household's own subscription. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Household mini's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Any household member can remove Household mini's access at any time from their Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
